Djengo Cyun-Jyun Fang

M.S. student in Machine Learning · Carnegie Mellon University

I am a master’s student in the Machine Learning Department at Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked with Prof. Andrea Zanette on harnesses in which frontier models write low-level robot skills as code. Formerly, I was a research assistant in the Embodied AI Lab at National Taiwan University working with Prof. Tsung-Wei Ke on LLM agents that seek information before they plan, and on reinforcement learning fine-tuning of vision-language-action models. I obtained my bachelor’s degrees in Computer Science and Information Engineering and Economics from National Taiwan University.

Research

My work focuses on multimodal language models and robot learning. Much of it aims at getting foundation models to act reliably in the physical world, where an agent has incomplete information and its assumptions about the world can be wrong. To these ends, I have worked on harnesses in which frontier models write robot control code, information-seeking LLM planners, and RL fine-tuning of vision-language-action models.

Can a Language Model Improve a Robot Policy by Editing Its Weights?

Experiment report, 2026

Frontier models read a policy network’s weights and rewrite them by hand, with no gradient training: simple faults get repaired, and one drawer-opening controller written from nothing reaches 99.85% success.
