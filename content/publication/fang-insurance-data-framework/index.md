Research and design work at [ABClab](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/en/Research/TeachingLab/-10281881), National Taiwan University, advised by Prof. Shih-Wei Liao: a unified data framework for insurance providers, and the report behind it. The framework was adopted by the [Life Insurance Association of the Republic of China](https://www.lia-roc.org.tw) to launch a wallet app integrating Taiwan's top 20 insurance providers.
