<p class="hero-role">M.S. student in Machine Learning · Carnegie Mellon University</p>

I am a master's student in the [Machine Learning Department](https://www.ml.cmu.edu/) at Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked with Prof. [Andrea Zanette](https://azanette.com) on harnesses in which frontier models write low-level robot skills as code. Formerly, I was a research assistant in the [Embodied AI Lab](https://embodiedai-ntu.github.io/) at National Taiwan University working with Prof. [Tsung-Wei Ke](https://twke18.github.io) on LLM agents that seek information before they plan, and on reinforcement learning fine-tuning of vision-language-action models. I obtained my bachelor's degrees in [Computer Science and Information Engineering](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/?locale=en) and [Economics](https://econ.ntu.edu.tw/en/home-en) from National Taiwan University.
